# Citing VIPER

If VIPER contributes to a publication, please cite the model paper:

Gelfand, J. D., Slane, P. O., & Zhang, W. 2009, ApJ, 703, 2051.
[doi:10.1088/0004-637X/703/2/2051](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/703/2/2051)

```bibtex
@ARTICLE{Gelfand2009,
  author  = {{Gelfand}, J.~D. and {Slane}, P.~O. and {Zhang}, W.},
  title   = {A Dynamical Model for the Evolution of a Pulsar Wind
             Nebula Inside a Nonradiative Supernova Remnant},
  journal = {The Astrophysical Journal},
  year    = 2009,
  volume  = {703},
  number  = {2},
  pages   = {2051--2067},
  doi     = {10.1088/0004-637X/703/2/2051}
}
```

The SNR dynamics follow
- Truelove & McKee (1999), ApJS, 120, 299. [doi:10.1086/313176](https://doi.org/10.1086/313176)
- Chevalier (1982), ApJ, 258, 790. [doi:10.1086/160126](https://doi.org/10.1086/160126)
- Bandiera (1984), A&A, 139, 368. [ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1984A%26A...139..368B)

The PWN dynamics follow
- Ostriker & Gunn (1971), ApJ, 164, L95. [doi:10.1086/180699](https://doi.org/10.1086/180699)
- Chevalier & Fransson (1992), ApJ, 395, 540. [doi:10.1086/171674](https://doi.org/10.1086/171674)

The radiation calculations follow
- Blumenthal & Gould (1970), Rev. Mod. Phys., 42, 237. [doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.42.237](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.42.237)
- Atoyan & Aharonian (1996), MNRAS, 278, 525. [doi:10.1093/mnras/278.2.525](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/278.2.525)

Applications of the model include:

- Alford, Zhang & Gelfand (2025), ApJ, 987, 63. [doi:10.3847/1538-4357/add92e](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/add92e)
- Gelfand, Slane & Temim (2015), ApJ, 807, 30. [doi:10.1088/0004-637X/807/1/30](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/807/1/30)
